Page layout

Use the page layout controls to choose the paper size, orientation, and margins for a PDF.

  • Paper size: Choose the dimensions of the output pages.
  • Orientation: Choose portrait or landscape.
  • Margins: Adjust the space around the contents of each page. Each margin stores its own measurement and unit. Changing page-size families converts the four margins to the new preferred unit.

Check the generated PDF preview to see how layout changes affect pagination.
